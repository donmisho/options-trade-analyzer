## Relevant Context — Do Not Deviate Without Escalation

Source: `insight_engine.md` §4.1 (Phase 4: Scoring)
- Each active scoring criterion produces a value in `[0, 100]`, multiplied by its junction `weight`. The weighted sum is the raw score. Weights across a strategy's active scoring criteria **must sum to 1.0**; the loader rejects any strategy that does not (§6.6).

Source: `insight_engine-schema-ddl.md` §2 (junction `weight`)
- `weight numeric(7,4)` lives on the junction. Variable params go in the junction `parameters` JSON. The live indicator totals active scoring weights and flags out-of-tolerance before save.

Source: `UI-GUIDANCE.md` Part 4 (formatting)
- Weights/scores formatted `##.00`. The total indicator uses score colors (and `.total.bad` → `var(--red)` per the prototype) when out of tolerance. No `$` prefix. Tokens only; `var(--bg2)` never on rows/headers.
