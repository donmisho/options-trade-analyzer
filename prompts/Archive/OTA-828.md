Source: OTA-791 (draft mechanism) / OTA-784 (selector shell)
Fact: The save-to-draft mechanism creates `<key>__draft` strategy rows with `status='draft'` and `owner_app_id='OTA'`. These are the editing substrate for in-progress edits — they are NOT user-selectable strategies. The admin list endpoint (OTA-823, consumed by `getAdminStrategies` in `client.js`) currently returns them, so they leak into the selector.

Boundary: OTA-790 owns *deleting* the `__draft` row after a successful Apply. THIS story owns *hiding* any draft row that exists in the list (which covers the mid-edit case OTA-790's post-Apply delete does not). Do not add delete logic here; filtering only.
