## Relevant Context — Do Not Deviate Without Escalation

**Source: `insight_engine.md` §1 — health grading is its own consumer.**
"every distinct evaluation surface (for OTA: trade screening, position health grading, directional thesis comparison) is its own consumer of the same engine." Health grading has its own adapter, strategies, and verdict bands (`A/B/C/D/F`, §3.8) — but the **same** engine and the **same** single path.

**Source: ticket OTA-764 — agent flow.**
For each open position the agent: (1) checks `position_exit_levels_complete` (published by F10 / OTA-734); (2) selects strategy — `position_health_full` when complete, `position_health_basic` otherwise; (3) calls `engine.evaluate([position_candidate], strategy_id, source_app_id="OTA")` against the `position_health` adapter; (4) persists the returned verdict **letter** to `positions.health_grade` (the full per-rule trace is already persisted by the OTA-758 sink); (5) keeps existing alert/insight escalation — invokes the renamed **Insight Communicator** only on threshold-crossing verdicts.

**Source: `insight_engine.md` §10 — naming.**
The old `app/agents/insight_engine.py` is the **Insight Communicator** (prose generation). Escalation logic stays in the agent; the engine produces verdicts; the Communicator produces prose. Confirm in Phase 0 whether the rename has landed; if not, preserve existing Communicator wiring rather than depending on a rename.

**Source: `insight_engine.md` §4.2 / §4.3 — record + persistence.**
`positions.health_grade` is written from the result record's verdict letter; the engine's sink owns trace persistence — the agent does not write the trace itself.

**Functional acceptance (reframe-consistent).** F11 (OTA-742) fixture-position health-letter expectations are the acceptance gate. These fixtures encode the **intended** health-grading behavior (not legacy parity); matching them is a functional check, consistent with the functional-not-parity standard.
