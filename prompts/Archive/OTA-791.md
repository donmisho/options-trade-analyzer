## Relevant Context — Do Not Deviate Without Escalation

Source: OTA-781 key design decision (save-to-draft)
- Edits persist to a draft strategy row: `status=draft`, reserved key e.g. `<key>__draft`. Preview reads that draft **fresh from the tables** and evaluates it. Apply (OTA-790) promotes the draft onto the live strategy key. Config always comes from the tables — **never** a client-injected ad-hoc config object.

Source: `insight_engine.md` §6.5 (restart-only reload)
- The engine is restart-only. Table changes take effect at next engine start. The **only** new engine capability here is "load one strategy fresh from tables for preview" — a narrow read, not general hot-reload, and not a mutation of the running engine's loaded config.

Source: `insight_engine.md` §6.2 (tables are runtime source)
- Preview config is sourced only from the tables. The client never passes a config payload into the engine; it edits the draft row, and preview re-reads that row.

Source: `insight_engine-schema-ddl.md` §1/§2 (app-scoped identity)
- The draft is an OTA-owned row (`owner_app_id='OTA'`); the reserved draft key must remain unique under `(owner_app_id, strategy_key)`.

Source: `UI-GUIDANCE.md` Part 4
- Preview results render scores `##.00` and verdicts via the standard verdict/score colors; dates `mm-dd-yyyy` via `formatDate()`; no `$` prefix.
